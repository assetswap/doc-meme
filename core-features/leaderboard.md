## 📊 How It Works

### Competition Period
- **Start**: Monday 00:00 UTC
- **End**: Sunday 22:00 UTC (10 PM)
- **Duration**: 7 days

### Ranking Calculation
Your position is determined by your **7-day trading volume** in USD for meme tokens:
- Only completed trades count
- Buy, sell, and swap transactions are included
- Only meme token trades qualify
- Volume is calculated in USD at the time of trade
